SyntecxHub Sales Dashboard 📊

 Project Overview

The **SyntecxHub Sales Dashboard** is an interactive sales analytics project designed to provide a clear view of business performance across products, sales channels, customer segments, categories, and time.

The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful insights that can support better business decisions, including inventory planning, sales strategy, product prioritization, and cost management.

🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project was to analyze sales performance and build an interactive dashboard that helps stakeholders:

- Monitor revenue and profit performance
- Identify the best-performing product categories
- Compare sales across different channels
- Track monthly sales and profit trends
- Identify top-selling products
- Understand customer segment performance
- Support data-driven business decisions

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
- DAX – Calculations and measures
- Data Visualization – Charts, KPIs, slicers and interactive visuals
- Forecast - Predict sale in the next 12 months

 🔍 Key Insights

1. Electronics is the strongest-performing category, generating approximately $2.1M in sales.

2. Online sales lead the sales channels, generating approximately $1.1M, slightly ahead of Retail Store and Wholesale.

3. The business generated approximately $990.3K in net profit, showing strong overall profitability.

4. **Laptops and Smartphones are the top-selling products, generating approximately $0.53M and $0.52M respectively.

5. Sales and profit fluctuate across the months, with October standing out as one of the stronger-performing periods.

💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

- Increase investment and inventory allocation toward the Electronics category.
- Strengthen the Online sales channel through targeted digital marketing and promotions.
- Maintain sufficient stock of Laptops and Smartphones to prevent stock-outs.
- Investigate the factors behind strong-performing months such as October and replicate successful strategies.
- Review major cost areas and identify opportunities to reduce unnecessary expenses and improve profit margins.
